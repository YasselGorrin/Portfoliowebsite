I've studied graphic design since high school and graduated from New World School of the Arts with my bachelor's in 2025 and have been working in the field for the last 5 years. I hold creativity & innovation to a high regard and think design/art has the power to change the world. My mission as a designer is to connect people and inspire others through my work. I am always looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow as a designer. When I'm not designing, I'm typically engaging in varieties of physical activities, reading, or spending quality time with my loved ones.

Fisherman Album Cover

I knew Manny David since Highschool and we both had a passion for creating things and bringing them to life. Manny is someone that has always supported my work and has always given me his thoughts about my projects, so when he told me that he was making an album I was more than happy to design his cover Fisherman I made multiple different covers so there a was a varity to choose from, I didnt want him to feel stuck in only choosing one or sway the decisions to one particular design, I wanted his involvement and thoughts on it, that 9/10 always produces the projects I enjoy the most.
